DriveSync

Two-way Google Drive folder sync for Linux

DriveSync keeps folders on your Linux computer in sync with folders in your Google Drive. It runs on your own computer, built on rclone, with a desktop app to manage folder pairs and a tray indicator that shows sync status.

Features

  • Two-way sync between local folders and Google Drive folders
  • Syncs on a schedule and when local files change
  • Review changes before they are applied, including moved and deleted files
  • Tray indicator showing whether each folder is synced, syncing or has an error
  • No servers, accounts or tracking: everything stays between your computer and Google

Get it

Source code and install instructions: github.com/dhendiii/drivesync

Privacy

DriveSync only accesses the Google Drive folders you choose to sync, and never sends your data to the developer. Read the full privacy policy.
